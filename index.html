How to race

Hold W or ↑ to accelerate. Brake with S or ↓, and steer with A / D or the left and right arrows. Use R to recover, T to restart all stages, C to change camera and SPACE to pause. On touchscreens, use the pedals.

Beat the clock

Each checkpoint picks up one rider and subtracts 2 seconds from your time. Pick up all three riders before you finish each stage. Reach the ferry in Stage 5 for the crew celebration.
